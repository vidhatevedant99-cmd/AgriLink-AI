# AgriLink AI 🌾

AI-powered agricultural market intelligence and farmer-buyer linkage platform.

## Problem

Farmers often lack transparent information about market prices,
buyer demand, logistics, storage and expected returns.

## Goal

AgriLink AI aims to help farmers and FPOs make better selling
decisions by combining market intelligence, AI-based price analysis,
buyer matching and logistics information.

## Current Focus

- Market price comparison
- Expected net realization
- Market recommendation
- Price prediction
- Buyer matching
- FPO aggregation
